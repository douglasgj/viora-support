Viora Support

Help with Viora Stream Player, your private organizer and player for authorized M3U and M3U8 media sources.

Add a media source

Open Playlists, choose Add Playlist, then enter a valid M3U or M3U8 address supplied by a service you are authorized to use.

Restore Viora Pro

Open the Viora Pro screen and select Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.

Playback help

Confirm that the source is online and that your device has a working internet connection. Stream availability and format support are controlled by the source provider.

Privacy
