Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

Viora Stream Player (“Viora”) is a private organizer and player for media sources that users are authorized to access. Viora does not provide, host, sell, or endorse media or playlists.

Information stored on your device

Playlist addresses and credentials are protected using the secure storage supplied by your device, including Apple Keychain on Apple platforms and Android Keystore on Android. Library indexes, favorites, playback history, subtitle information, preferences, and resume progress are stored locally on your device. Viora uses an encrypted local library cache. This information is not uploaded to a Viora-operated server.

Media sources

When you add a source, Viora connects directly to the address you provide to retrieve its playlist, metadata, artwork, guide information, and media streams. The source provider can receive technical network information normally sent during an internet request, including your IP address. The source provider’s own privacy practices apply.

Advertising and consent

Some platform versions of Viora may display advertising supplied by Google Mobile Ads. Depending on your platform, region, consent choices, and device privacy permissions, Google and its partners may process approximate location, device identifiers, product interactions, advertising data, crash data, and performance data to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, analyze performance, and improve reliability. You can manage available consent and privacy controls in Viora and your device settings.

Purchases

Viora Pro purchases and subscriptions are processed by the platform store, such as Apple or Google Play. Viora receives entitlement status needed to unlock purchased features but does not receive or store your full payment-card details.

Children

Viora is not designed to collect personal information from children. Parents and guardians should supervise the sources added to the app and use the available parent-PIN protection where appropriate.

Retention and deletion

You can remove a playlist and its locally stored library information from within Viora. Deleting the app removes its local app data, subject to platform behavior for secure-storage items and device backups.

Your choices

  • Control available privacy permissions in your device settings.
  • Remove playlists, history, favorites, and cached data from the app.
  • Contact a source provider regarding information processed by that provider.
  • Contact Viora support with a privacy request.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Viora changes. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest version.
